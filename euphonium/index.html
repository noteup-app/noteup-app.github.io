About NoteUp!

NoteUp! is a free practice app for everyone who makes music. You practise rhythm reading by tapping along, ear training with intervals, melodies and tuning by ear, scales with their seventh chord and intonation. Everything runs right in your browser, without an account. NoteUp! is free and collects no user data.
